# Account setup and verification (the user does the typing, you guide)

LinkedIn policy in plain words: one person, one account, real name. Fake profiles and second accounts get restricted. If anything here conflicts with what LinkedIn shows on screen today, trust LinkedIn's screen and official help pages.

## Step 0: Check first
Ask: "Do you already have a LinkedIn account?" (yes / no / not sure). If not sure: search the user's email for messages from LinkedIn, and try "Forgot password" on linkedin.com. Do not create a second account if one exists.

## Step 1: Create the account (user types everything personal)
1. Go to linkedin.com/signup in the user's own browser.
2. Email (or phone) and password: the user chooses and types them. A password manager is a good idea. You never see them.
3. First and last name: their real name, the way colleagues know them.
4. Location and a recent job title or "student". Add a company only if true.
5. LinkedIn may send a verification code to their email or phone. They type it in. Never ask them to read it to you.
6. A CAPTCHA or puzzle may appear. The user solves it. You do not.
7. Skip "import contacts" unless they want it. Say what it does (uploads their address book) before they choose.

## Step 2: Verification and security (the user does these)
- Email verified: open the message and click LinkedIn's link or use the code.
- Phone number: optional; helps recovery and trust. Their choice.
- Two-step verification: Settings > Sign in & security > Two-step verification. Recommend turning it on.
- ID or selfie verification: only if LinkedIn asks, and only inside LinkedIn's own pages. Explain what it is. Never ask them to paste an ID, selfie or document number into the chat.
- If LinkedIn restricts the account: use LinkedIn's official help and appeal form. No workarounds.

## Step 3: First-day profile basics (drafts first)
Photo, headline (draft 3 options), location, current role or study, education with real dates, 5 skills. Turn "Notify network" off while editing. Do not post or send invitations on day one: build the profile, then connect with people they already know.

## Step 4: Privacy settings to review together
Profile visibility, who can see their connections, who can find them by email or phone, and whether to show "open to work" to everyone or recruiters only (their choice).

## Step 5: Then continue with the interview (Phase 1)

## Never
- Never type, view or store a password, code, recovery key, ID or card number.
- Never create an account in someone else's name, or a fake or duplicate account.
- Never bypass or solve a CAPTCHA or a bot check.
- Never create the account unless the account owner asked you to help and is watching.
