# Job-search plan (honest, no guarantees)

## 1. Target
- 2 to 3 target titles, 3 company types, a location/remote rule, a salary range (private).
- A list of 20 target companies, split into: dream, realistic, safe. The user chooses; do not invent companies' details, check them.

## 2. Make the story consistent
CV, LinkedIn headline, About and Experience should tell the same story and use the same dates. Fix mismatches before applying.

## 3. Interview story bank (situation, action, result)
Build 6 stories from `story-bank.md`: a win, a failure and what changed, a conflict handled calmly, a thing you led, a thing you learned fast, a thing you are proud of. Real numbers only.

## 4. Weekly routine (adjust to the time they have)
- 3 tailored applications (not 30 generic ones).
- 3 human conversations: a former colleague, a recruiter, someone in the target role.
- 2 posts or 3 thoughtful comments.
- 1 follow-up round.
- 10-minute review on Friday.

## 5. Outreach scripts (rewrite in their voice, never mass-send)
- To a recruiter: who they are, the role family they target, one proof point, a question about fit.
- To someone in the role: one true line from their profile, a specific question, no ask for a job.
- Follow-up after 7 days, once. After that, stop.
Check the open thread's name before every send.

## 6. Tracker (private, local only)
Table: company, role, date, contact (private), stage, next step, date of next step.
Never publish it. Never put it in a shared or public repository.

## 7. Offers and decisions
When an offer arrives, compare against what they said matters in the interview (growth, money, flexibility, people, purpose). Say what is unknown. The decision is theirs.

## 8. Be honest about timing
Job searches are uneven. Report what is working, what is not, and the next small experiment. Never promise a date or an outcome.
