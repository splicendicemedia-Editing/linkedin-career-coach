# Post templates (use their real stories only)

General: first line is the hook. Short lines. One idea per post. End with one honest question. Three hashtags at most. Vary length week to week. Never reuse one structure on consecutive posts.

## 1. The moment (story)
Hook: the surprising moment, in one line.
Body: what happened first, what went wrong, what changed.
Lesson: one sentence.
Question: "What would you have done?"

## 2. The lesson
Hook: "I used to believe X. I don't anymore."
Body: why they believed it, what changed their mind (a real event).
Close: what they do now.

## 3. The how-to (3 steps)
Hook: the result in one line.
Steps 1, 2, 3 in short lines, each from something they actually did.
Question: "What would you add?"

## 4. The honest position
Hook: a view some people will argue with.
Body: why they hold it, what it costs them.
Close: invite the other side politely.

## 5. The behind-the-scenes
A small real piece of their week or process. No confidential details.

## Fact gate (run before showing any draft)
- Every number came from the user (or a document they gave).
- Every named person, employer or client is allowed in `settings.md`.
- No personal data (family, health, salary) unless they chose to share it.
- No claim that cannot be backed up.
- AI help is not hidden if asked directly.

## Comments (on other people's posts)
Add one specific thing: a short story, a question, a respectful disagreement, or a useful fact. Avoid "Great post!". Do not repeat the same structure twice in a row. They approve each comment unless they chose likes-only automation.

## Replies to comments on their posts
Reply within a day, by name, with one real sentence. They approve unless they decided otherwise.
