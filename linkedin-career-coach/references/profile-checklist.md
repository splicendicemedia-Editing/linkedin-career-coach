# Profile makeover checklist (drafts first, apply only with approval)

Order of impact: headline, About, current role, photo, Featured, skills, URL, recommendations.

## Headline (220 characters max)
Formula: [Who you help or what you do] | [proof or specialty] | [current role or company].
- Say what you do for others, not only a job title.
- Use words a recruiter would search for.
- No buzzword pile ("passionate", "guru", "ninja").
Give 3 options and let the user pick.

## About (first 3 lines matter most)
1. Line 1: the problem you solve, or a true one-sentence hook from their story bank.
2. Short paragraph: what you do and for whom.
3. Two or three proof points (real numbers only).
4. What you are looking for next, in plain words.
5. One clear way to contact them.
Keep short lines. No made-up claims.

## Experience
- One to three bullets per role: action + result. Use a number only if the user gave it.
- Match titles and dates to the CV. Never invent responsibilities.
- Add skills to the roles that used them.

## Education and dates
Only what the user states. Ask for month/year if unsure; a year alone is fine.

## Photo and banner
Clear face, good light, plain background, shoulders up. Banner: one line of what they do, or a clean image. Suggest, do not edit images without being asked.

## Featured section
Best post, a project, a certificate or a case study. Only items they own and approve.

## Skills
Top 3 pinned skills = the roles they want. 15 to 30 total. Ask before adding any.

## Custom URL
firstname-lastname if available.

## Safety when applying edits
- Check the signed-in name first.
- Edit one section at a time and re-read the page afterwards.
- Turn "Notify network" OFF unless they want the announcement.
- Decline LinkedIn's "post this update" prompt unless they ask for it.
